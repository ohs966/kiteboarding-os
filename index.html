03 · The lagoon

Learn the Old School way.

OS stands for the Old School. It means patient instructors, safety before speed, and a shallow, flat lagoon where your feet can touch the bottom while you learn. Private or small-group lessons, at your pace.

Included
Wetsuit, shoes, helmet and life jacket.

Book with a deposit only. Kids join free with a paying adult.

Film: a kitesurfer rides across shallow, flat water and jumps, a windsurfer sailing behind.

04 · South Sinai

Between the mountains and the reef.

Sharm El Sheikh sits at the southern tip of the Sinai Peninsula, where desert mountains run straight down to the Red Sea. Our school is in Nabq Bay, north of town.

  1. Sharm El Sheikh

05 · The boat

Egypt is your playground.

Not every holiday needs a lesson. Grab the family and spend an hour on the boat: banana boat, sofa tube, parasailing, or try wakeboarding and water skiing behind the boat. Calm-day option: paddle the lagoon on a SUP or kayak.

Kids join free with a paying adult.

06 · Rental

Bring your skills. We have the kit.

Rent by the hour, half day or full day. Wetsuit, shoes, helmet and life jacket included. Storage and launch from the school.

Kites from Ozone, North, Cabrinha, Duotone, Eleveight and RRD. Boards and wings from North, Eleveight, F-One, RRD and Airush. Windsurf gear from Fanatic, JP and NeilPryde. Also ask us about Core, Naish, Slingshot, Flysurfer, Starboard and Severne.
